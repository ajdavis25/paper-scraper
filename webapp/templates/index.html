get started

prefer a guided walkthrough? the new get started tab walks new accounts through the same setup flow below so you can start with solid defaults in just a few clicks.

  1. subscribe or sign up: drop your email on the subscribe page or create an account so you can tweak everything from the dashboard, then pop over to the get started tab for a guided setup.
  2. set your filters: users can visit preferences to add keywords, authors, and arXiv categories that matter to you.
  3. train it: check recommendations or your dashboard and start liking / disliking papers so the digest learns your taste.
  4. read the daily digest: every morning you'll get a curated email of new papers — keep reacting to tune future picks.
