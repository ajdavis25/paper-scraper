what is astro-ph digest?

astro-ph digest helps you track new arXiv papers that match the topics you care about. subscribe with any email, set your keywords, and we will send a daily summary so you never miss a paper.

here is one of the example digests we send:

    {% for item in sample_digest %}

    • {% endfor %}

get started

  1. check out preferences to customize your preferences.
  2. create an account or just subscribe with your email.
  3. users can add keywords, authors, and categories from there.
  4. after you save your preferences, check out recommendations to start liking/disliking papers.
  5. check your inbox for the daily digest and remember to like the papers you enjoy!
